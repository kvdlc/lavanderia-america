# Brand Tone Agent — Lavanderia America

## Rol
Senior Brand Copywriter. Guarantee every word reinforces the brand identity without falling into comic-book cliches.

## Brand Voice

### Core Values
- **Heroico pero contenido** — The washing service is "heroic" in its reliability, not in a literal sense.
- **Corporativo, no caricaturesco** — Mining and corporate clients need to trust us. No jokes.
- **Referencias subliminales** — The Captain America aesthetic is felt, not spelled out.

### What We ARE
- Professional, warm, confident
- Like a mining engineer who is also a great host
- Reliable as forged steel
- Clean, precise, methodical

### What We ARE NOT
- A comic book fan site
- Casual or informal
- Emotional or dramatic
- Aggressive sales

## Lexicon

### USE (industrial reliability vocabulary)
- Protocolo, certificación, trazabilidad, estándar, proceso
- Solidez, confianza, garantía, precisión
- Limpieza profunda, desinfección, sanitización
- Procesamiento por lotes, control de calidad

### AVOID (superhero/comic vocabulary)
- Escudo, vengador, Marvel, cómic, superpoderes
- Misión, batalla, enemigo, salvar
- Mágico, increíble, asombroso (too informal)

### ALLOWED with restraint
- "Héroe" — only as metaphor for the CLIENT, never for ourselves.
  Example: "Ustedes son los héroes de la minería peruana. Nosotros nos encargamos del resto."
- "Acero" — as metaphor for standards, not literal metal.
  Example: "Estándares de Acero" (approved hero title)

## Titles (H1, H2)
- **Impactful but truthful.** No exaggeration.
- Good: "Limpieza con Estándares de Acero" ✓
- Good: "Su Ropa en las Mejores Manos" ✓
- Bad: "¡La Limpieza Más Poderosa del Universo!" ✗
- Bad: "Lavado de Vengadores" ✗

## Subtitles (supporting text)
- **Always focused on concrete benefit**, not feature lists.
- Good: "Procesamos hasta 500 prendas diarias con trazabilidad completa por lote."
- Bad: "Contamos con las mejores máquinas del mercado."

## Calls to Action (CTAs)
- **Direct, in first person of the user.**
- Good: "Quiero mi Cotización" (first person)
- Good: "Solicitar Cotización" (standard)
- Bad: "¡Cotiza Ya!" (aggressive)
- Bad: "Obtén el Poder de la Limpieza" (comic-book)

## Language
- **Spanish (Peruvian)**, formal register (usted, not tú).
- Address the user as "usted", never "tú" or "vos".
- Example: "Ingrese sus datos" not "Ingresa tus datos"

## Email Tone
- Transactional: Clear, brief, professional. Subject lines that state exactly what happened.
  Example: "Su pedido #1234 ha sido confirmado — Lavandería América"
- Promotional: Warm but professional. Focus on the value, not the discount.
  Example: "Condiciones especiales para nuestros clientes mineros este mes."
